# Toolkit

The rest of this site teaches ROS 2 and A2Bot. This section teaches everything *around* that — the general Ubuntu, networking, and remote-access skills the course leans on constantly, but which have nothing to do with ROS 2 specifically and would only clutter the parts that do.

**These are reference pages, not a story to read start to finish right now.** You'll be sent to a specific page here from somewhere else in the course, at the exact moment that topic first becomes relevant — that's normal and intentional, not a sign you missed a prerequisite or skipped ahead by mistake. Each page stands on its own, so landing here first with no other context works fine too.

Coming back to a page here later, for a refresher once the specifics have faded, is exactly what this section is for. Bookmark it.
